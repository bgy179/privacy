# SounDial Privacy Policy **Last updated: September 3, 2026** SounDial ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SounDial handles information when you use the application. ## 1. Information We Access SounDial may access the following device features and data when necessary to provide its functionality: ### Bluetooth SounDial uses Bluetooth to discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible Bluetooth devices. Bluetooth-related information is used only for the operation of the Bluetooth functionality within the App. ### Microphone SounDial may access the microphone when you use features that require audio input or audio communication. Audio captured through the microphone is processed for the functionality requested by you. SounDial does not upload microphone recordings to a server. ### Files SounDial may access files on your device when you choose to open, select, send, receive, or otherwise process files using the App. SounDial only accesses files when necessary for features initiated by you. ## 2. Data Collection SounDial does **not operate its own server** and does not collect or maintain personal information on a remote server. SounDial does not intentionally collect: - Your name - Email address - Phone number - Precise location - Contacts - Account credentials - Personal profile information The App does not require you to create an account. ## 3. Data Storage SounDial does not transmit your personal information to a SounDial-operated server. Data generated or processed by the App may remain locally on your device, depending on how you use the App and the features you select. You are responsible for files and other content that you choose to provide to the App. ## 4. Data Sharing SounDial does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. SounDial does not use personal information for advertising purposes. ## 5. Network Communication SounDial does not operate a backend server for collecting or storing user information. If the App communicates with another device or service as part of a feature you explicitly use, that communication is limited to providing the requested functionality. ## 6. Permissions Depending on your device and the features you use, SounDial may request permissions related to: - Bluetooth - Microphone - Files and media You can manage these permissions through your device's system settings. You may deny or revoke permissions, although doing so may prevent certain features of SounDial from functioning correctly. ## 7. Children's Privacy SounDial does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because SounDial does not require an account and does not operate a server for collecting personal information, the App does not maintain a database of children's personal information. ## 8. Security SounDial does not operate a server that stores users' personal information. We nevertheless recommend that you use the security features provided by your device and keep your operating system up to date. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. ## 10. Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SounDial, please contact us through the contact information provided on the SounDial application listing or its official website. --- **SounDial** **Last updated: September 3, 2026**
